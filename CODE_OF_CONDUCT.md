# Code of Conduct

LeanTTY contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, professional and focused on the work.

Acceptable participation includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence respectfully;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- protecting other people's private information; and
- respecting project scope and maintainer decisions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized content, deliberate
disruption, doxxing and publication of another person's credentials or private
data are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community and project.

Do not open a public issue about another participant. Use GitHub's
[Report abuse](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) process for
abusive content. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.
